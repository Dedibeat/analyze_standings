Virtual-contest performance calculator

Pick the contest you ran virtually, tick the problems you solved (penalty in minutes optional), and get your standing against the contest's real field plus an ICPC East Asia medal estimate. Works for every fitted contest with standings; EA medal contests additionally rank you inside the official (medal-eligible) field. Your performance bar is the difficulty at which your solve indicator crosses 50% — the same isotonic crossing that defines the medal bars — so it transfers across contests on the shared difficulty scale (Codeforces-equivalent points).

East Asia medal projection

Your performance bar vs each EA medal contest's cutoff bars (the difficulty its medal-boundary cohort solves at even odds, CF points). Gold where your bar clears the gold bar, and so on; the grade column places you on the badge ladder bronze < bronze+ < silver < silver+ < gold < gold+ < plat < plat+.

Generated by arch_b.export_performance_viewer from the survival-model fit (problem_ratings_calibrated.json), the raw standings, and medal_badges.json. Caveats:
  • The bar quantizes on one contest's solve/miss pattern (~13 binary observations) and ignores penalty — for the contest you actually ran, the direct rank insertion is the authoritative number; the projection is for transferring to the other regionals.
  • Rank insertion counts teams that solved ≥1 problem; without a penalty you get the best–worst range over the block of teams with your solve count, and a team with an unknown penalty is assumed to finish ahead of you.
  • Medal cutoffs use ceil rounding of the 10/30/60% percentiles of official solving teams; hosts may adjust boundaries at ties.
