ICPC East Asia — medal badges

Asia East Continent regionals award medals by cumulative percentile of official teams solving at least one problem: gold 10%, silver 30%, bronze 60%. Each problem is badged with the weakest grade whose anchor cohort solves it at ≥50% odds, measured on standings-fitted difficulties (UCup-anchored survival model, Codeforces-equivalent points). The ladder is bronze < bronze+ < silver < silver+ < gold < gold+ < plat < plat+: each medal's “+” edge is the medal cutoff itself and its plain edge the mid-band cohort (20% / 45% / 80% — plain bronze is the giveaways the whole field solves). Above the gold-medal bar sits platinum, split at the champion cohort (the top-5 official teams): still solvable by the champions at even odds → plat (decides ranking within gold); beyond even the champions → plat+. Official teams are identified by matching XCPCIO scoreboard data against the qoj standings.

Generated by arch_b.export_medal_viewer from output/medal_badges.json (analysis: arch_b.medals). Caveats:
  • Medal counts use ceil rounding of the 10/30/60% cutoffs; the official rounding rule is not in the data, and hosts may adjust boundaries at ties.
  • The domjudge field is taken as the medal-eligible field — fuzzy for COVID-era online contests (Hong Kong & Macau 2022's “official” field includes non-EA online teams, which is why its gold bar is the outlier).
  • Xi'an 2025 was declared unrated; it is included with its qoj standings as-is.
  • A model-free sanity badge (weakest half-band of official ranks where ≥50% of teams solved; top-5 majority splits plat/plat+) agrees on ~73% of problems; disagreements are almost all one grade easier, as expected when anchoring on the band majority instead of the boundary cohort.
  • The plat bar leans on a 5-team champion cohort, so it is the noisiest of the bars — in small fields (e.g. Xi'an 2023, 34 officials) it can sit close to the gold-medal bar and badge a several-solve problem plat+.
