Problem J. Strange Sum

Given a sequence a1 , a2 , . . . , an .
You are going to select zero or more elements of a so that: if you select ai , then in any interval of length
i (formally, in a[j, j + i − 1] for any 1 ≤ j ≤ n − i + 1) you can select at most 2 elements.
Calculate the maximal sum of the elements you select.

Input
The first line contains an integer n (2 ≤ n ≤ 105 ).
The second line contains n integers a1 , a2 , . . . , an (−109 ≤ ai ≤ 109 ).

Output
Output a single integer denoting the answer.
