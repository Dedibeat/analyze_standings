Froggy has an unrooted tree T with n vertices, where each vertex has an initial weight. Initially, the
weight of vertex i is i.
Froggy can perform at most 1 operation sequentially as follows:
• Select several distinct vertices x1 , x2 , · · · , xk , such that for i = 1, 2, · · · , k − 1, there exists an edge
in tree T connecting vertex xi and vertex xi+1 .
• From the set x1 , x2 , · · · , xk , choose an even number of vertices u1 , u2 , · · · , u2l (vertices can be chosen
repeatedly). For i = 1, 2, · · · , l, sequentially swap the weights of vertex u2i−1 and vertex u2i .
Let ai denote the weight of vertex i after all operations. How many distinct sequences a1 , a2 , · · · , an can
Froggy obtain? Output the answer modulo 998244353.

Input
The first line contains a positive integer T (1 ≤ T ≤ 103 ), indicating the number of test cases.
For each test case, the first line contains an integer n (1 ≤ n ≤ 3000), representing the number of vertices
in the tree.
The next n − 1 lines each contain two positive integers x, y (1 ≤ x, y ≤ n, x ̸= y), representing the two
endpoints of an edge in tree T .
It is guaranteed that the sum of n over all test cases in a single test point does not exceed 1.5 × 104 .

Output
For each test case, output one integer representing the answer modulo 998244353.
