The problem database of Budada is an integer sequence {ci }ni=1 . Each integer has a corresponding
Numerical difficulty, which is also an integer sequence {di }ni=1 . Please notice that ci are not necessarily

Budada wants to prepare the most difficult test for Putata, so he will choose two integers i, j such
