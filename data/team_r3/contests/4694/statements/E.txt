Problem E. Coffee Record
This is an interactive problem. Dew assigns a lowercase English letter to every cup of coffee she drinks, then concatenates these letters in the order in which she drinks the coffee. To look back on her coffee experiences, she wants to choose some consecutive cups from her record and divide them, in their original order, into three groups to form a coffee collection. She sets the following requirements and scoring rule. Let the current record be a string s. A coffee collection consists of a contiguous substring P of s, together with a way to split P into three nonempty strings A, B, C, so that
P =A+B+C
The strings P, A, B, C must all be palindromes. The score of a coffee collection satisfying this requirement is the number of cups in its smallest group, namely
min(|A|, |B|, |C|)
where |X| denotes the length of a string X. The optimal score of the current record is the maximum score among all coffee collections satisfying the requirement. If there is no such collection, the optimal score is 0. Dew would like you to help her find the optimal score of the current record after each character is appended to it. However, she does not want to reveal her coffee record to you directly, so you can only obtain the information you need by asking her questions. Specifically, the string s is initially empty, and the interaction consists of n rounds. In each round, one lowercase English letter is appended to the end of s; in round t, |s| = t. The contents of s are hidden from you. You may use the following two types of queries to obtain information:

In each round, you may make at most three queries in total across both types, after which you must report the optimal score of the current s.  For two strings A and B, A + B denotes the concatenation of A and B in this order. For example, butterfly + dew = butterflydew.
