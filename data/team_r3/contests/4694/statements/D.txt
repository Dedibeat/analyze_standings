Problem D. Travel Plan
Rose is a girl who enjoys traveling. She has arrived in a new city.
The city has n attractions, numbered 1  n, connected by m two-way roads. Each road connects two distinct attractions, and there is at most one road between any pair of attractions. Any attraction can be reached from any other attraction via a sequence of roads. In other words, all attractions and roads in the city form a simple connected undirected graph with n vertices and m edges.
Rose wants to start from one attraction and visit all attractions in depth-first search (DFS) order. Specifically, for every attraction u, she arranges the vertices reached by each road starting from u into an ordered list adj(u) in some order. She then starts from the selected starting vertex s and travels according to the following pseudocode:
