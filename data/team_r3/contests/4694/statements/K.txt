Problem K. Salary Adjustment

You are an employee of Incredibly Clever Pig Corporation (ICPC).
There are n employees in the corporation, numbered 1, 2, . . . , n. The superior-subordinate relationships among the employees form a rooted tree with employee 1 as the root. For i = 2, 3, . . . , n, the direct superior of employee i is employee fi. It is guaranteed that 1  fi < i. The corporation is about to adjust the salaries of all its employees. The current salary of employee i is ai, and the target salary is bi. The whole adjustment process lasts exactly n days: each day, exactly one employee who has not been adjusted yet is chosen, and on that day the salary of the chosen employee is changed directly from its current value to its target value. Therefore, the adjustment process corresponds to a permutation p = (p1, p2, . . . , pn) of 1 to n, where pk denotes the employee adjusted on the k-th day. However, the corporation has strict rules about salaries. Specifically, if employee u is a superior of employee v, then at any moment the salary of employee u must not be lower than the salary of employee v. It is guaranteed that both the salaries ai before the adjustment and the salaries bi after the adjustment satisfy this rule.
A permutation p corresponding to an adjustment process is legal if and only if this rule still holds after each day's adjustment is completed.
Please find the number of legal permutations p, modulo 998 244 353.
