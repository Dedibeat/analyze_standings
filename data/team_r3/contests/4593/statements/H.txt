Neko is fighting a long battle against the Ocean Gangster. The battle consists of a sequence of rounds,
all numbered by positive integers.
Neko has two defensive abilities: Metallization and Plating. Both abilities initially have level 0.
At the beginning of each round, Neko may perform any number of upgrades:
• Pay x to increase the Metallization level by 1.
• Pay y to increase the Plating level by 1.
After all upgrades have been performed, the round is resolved in the following order:
1. Let the current Metallization and Plating levels be M and P , respectively. Neko gains M + P points
of armor.
2. If the Ocean Gangster deals b points of damage in this round, Neko loses max(0, b − M − P ) health
points.
3. At the end of the round, all armor gained in this round disappears. The Metallization level remains
unchanged, while the Plating level becomes max(P − 1, 0).
The Ocean Gangster will make n attacks in total. The i-th attack occurs in round ai and deals bi points
of damage. The attack rounds are given in strictly increasing order. The Ocean Gangster does not attack
in any round that is not listed. Note that even if there is no attack in a round, the Plating level still
changes according to the rule above at the end of that round.
As a genius gaming streamer, Neko wants to lose no health points to any attack. Find the minimum total
cost required to achieve this goal.

Input
This problem contains multiple test cases.
The first line contains a positive integer T (1 ≤ T ≤ 5 × 105 ), representing the number of test cases.
For each test case:
• The first line contains three integers n, x, y (1 ≤ n ≤ 5 × 105 , 1 ≤ x, y ≤ 109 ), representing the
number of attacks, the cost of increasing the Metallization level by 1, and the cost of increasing the
Plating level by 1, respectively.
• The next n lines each contain two integers ai , bi (1 ≤ ai , bi ≤ 109 ), representing an attack of bi
damage made by the Ocean Gangster in round ai .
It is guaranteed that the sum of n over all test cases does not exceed 5 × 105 , and that a1 < a2 < · · · < an .

Output
For each test case, output one line containing an integer representing the minimum cost required to lose
no health points to any attack.

Note
In the first test case, Neko can first increase the Metallization level to 2 in round 1:
• In round 1, increase the Plating level to 1, obtaining 2 + 1 = 3 points of armor in total.
• In round 10, increase the Plating level to 3, obtaining 2 + 3 = 5 points of armor in total.
• In round 20, the 2 points of armor provided by Metallization are sufficient.
The total cost is 2 × 5 + (1 + 3) × 2 = 18.
In the second test case, Neko does not need to upgrade Metallization. In round 1, increase the Plating
level to 5. At the end of this round, its level decreases to 4. In each of rounds 2 and 3, increase the Plating
level by 1. This provides 5 points of armor in all three rounds, for a total cost of 5 + 1 + 1 = 7.
