The average of averages is not always the average.
VegiCat has just learned about segment trees and wants to practice using them.
For a positive integer n, he considers the sequence ai = i (1 ≤ i ≤ n). He decides to
build a segment tree to answer range average queries on this sequence.
In VegiCat’s segment tree, each node corresponds to an interval [l, r] and stores a
value vl,r , which VegiCat intends to represent the average over that interval. The
root corresponds to the entire interval [1, n].

nodes and divides the sum by 2.
