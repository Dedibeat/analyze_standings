Joint Relocation

Time limit: 4s

VegiCat owns a company with n employees in k nonempty teams. The employees live
along a straight road, represented by a number line. VegiCat wants to arrange two
meeting points for them.
VegiCat divides the teams into two sets, A and B, with each team in exactly one set.
He chooses a meeting point for each set at any real coordinate. All employees on
the same team must go to the same meeting point. Each team in set A goes
to the meeting point for A, and each team in set B goes to the meeting point for B.
Either set may be empty.
Let SA list the home positions of all employees on teams in A, and deﬁne SB similarly
for B. Each employee is counted separately, even when home positions repeat.
For a list S = (x1 , x2 , . . . , xm ) of home positions, the minimum total distance for these employees to travel to

Input
The ﬁrst line contains an integer T (1 ≤ T ≤ 3 · 105 ), the number of test cases. The description of the test cases
follows.
The ﬁrst line of each test case contains two integers n and k (1 ≤ k ≤ n ≤ 3 · 105 ), denoting the number of
employees and the number of teams, respectively.
Each of the next k lines describes one team. The i-th line contains an integer mi (1 ≤ mi ≤ n), the number of
employees on team i, followed by mi integers xi,1 , . . . , xi,mi (1 ≤ xi,j ≤ 107 ), their home positions. The sum of
all mi in a test case is n.
Home positions may repeat and may be given in any order.
It is guaranteed that the sum of n over all test cases does not exceed 3 · 105 .

Output
For each test case, output an integer — the minimum possible total travel distance.

position 8. The employees in each team travel a total distance of 2, giving the answer 4.
In the second test case, VegiCat chooses meeting points at 3 and 9. He sends teams 1 and 2 to the ﬁrst meeting
point, and team 3 to the second. The employees in teams 1, 2, and 3 travel total distances of 12, 1, and 2,
respectively, giving the answer 15.
