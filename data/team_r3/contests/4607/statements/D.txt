D

Duel of Wrath

Time limit: 1s

VegiCat and VegiDog are playing a special duel in Slay the Spire. VegiCat plays as
Watcher A, and VegiDog plays as Watcher B. A and B have base attack powers a and
b and initial health values hA and hB , respectively.
At the start of each round, the game determines each Watcher’s stance for that round.
Each Watcher can be in one of the following two stances:
• Normal: damage dealt and damage received are unchanged.
• Wrath: damage dealt and damage received are both doubled.
For each attack, the damage initially equals the attacker’s base attack power. It is
doubled if the attacker is in Wrath and doubled again if the defender is in Wrath.
Thus, the damage in each round is as follows:
• If both characters are in Normal, A deals a damage to B, and B deals b damage to A.
• If exactly one character is in Wrath, A deals 2a damage to B, and B deals 2b damage to A.
• If both characters are in Wrath, A deals 4a damage to B, and B deals 4b damage to A.
Each round, both characters attack once simultaneously, and both health values decrease simultaneously. Both
attacks take eﬀect even if either character’s health becomes 0 or less during the round. At the end of the
round, a character with health less than or equal to 0 dies. If either character dies, the battle ends immediately;
otherwise, the next round begins. Base attack powers never change, and health is never restored.
The stances for each round are determined by the game, not by the players. Any combination of the two
characters’ stances is possible in each round.
VegiCat wins only if B dies while A remains alive. If both characters die in the same round, VegiCat does not
win. Determine whether VegiCat will win regardless of how both characters’ stances change.

Input
The ﬁrst line contains an integer T (1 ≤ T ≤ 105 ), the number of test cases. The description of the test cases
follows.
Each test case contains one line with four integers a, b, hA , and hB (1 ≤ a, b, hA , hB ≤ 1018 ), giving the
characters’ base attack powers and initial health values, respectively.

Output
For each test case, output YES if VegiCat will win regardless of how both characters’ stances change, and NO
otherwise. Output each answer on a separate line.
You can output the answer in any case. For example, yEs, yes, Yes, and YES are all accepted as positive
responses.
