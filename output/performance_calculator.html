Virtual-contest performance calculator

Pick the contest you ran virtually, tick the problems you solved (penalty in minutes optional), and get your standing against the contest's real field plus an ICPC East Asia medal estimate. Works for every fitted contest with standings; EA medal contests additionally rank you inside the official (medal-eligible) field. Two channels, on two scales that are never mixed: your performance is the classic Elo rank-inversion of your inserted rank against the field's fitted abilities (raw scale), compared to the same number for each regional's medal-cutoff teams — the primary medal estimate, needing only solve count + penalty. Your performance bar is the difficulty at which your solve indicator crosses 50% (the crossing that defines the medal bars, CF-equivalent points) — the training view, using which problems you solved.

East Asia medal projection

Medal (perf) — the primary estimate — compares your Elo rank-inversion performance to each contest's gold/silver/bronze cutoff-team performances (raw scale; the gold column shows the performance the gold cutoff team posted). Medal (bar) compares your performance bar to the contest's medal bars (difficulty, CF points), with the grade column placing you on the badge ladder bronze < bronze+ < silver < silver+ < gold < gold+ < plat < plat+. The channels stay separate: performance vs performance, difficulty vs difficulty.

Generated by arch_b.export_performance_viewer from the survival-model fit (problem_ratings_calibrated.json), the raw standings, and medal_badges.json. Caveats:
  • The two channels are deliberately never mixed: the Elo rank-inversion performance is measured as inflated against the difficulty scale (see details.md), so it is only ever compared to the cutoff teams' performances, and the bar only to the medal bars. Where they disagree, the performance channel is the better medal estimate (finer-grained, uses penalty); the bar is the training view.
  • The bar quantizes on one contest's solve/miss pattern (~13 binary observations) and ignores penalty — for the contest you actually ran, the direct rank insertion is the authoritative number; the projection is for transferring to the other regionals.
  • Rank insertion counts teams that solved ≥1 problem; without a penalty you get the best–worst range over the block of teams with your solve count, and a team with an unknown penalty is assumed to finish ahead of you.
  • Medal cutoffs use ceil rounding of the 10/30/60% percentiles of official solving teams; hosts may adjust boundaries at ties.
