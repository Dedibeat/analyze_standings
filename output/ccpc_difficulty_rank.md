# CCPC sets ranked by problem difficulty

Generated by `scripts/ccpc_difficulty_rank.py`. Spoiler-free: ranks and verdicts only.

Rank 1 = hardest: fewest problems rated <= 2400 CF; ties go to the set where a 2400-rated team expects fewer solves. Verdicts compare with the mainland ICPC EC regionals of 2023-2025: "harder" / "easier" = outside the middle half of those regionals, else "same".

- **Medal harder?** The performance of the last bronze / silver / gold medallist (onsite field and problems together). n/a: no onsite board.
- **Problems harder?** The rating needed to solve as many problems as on a typical regional set, for a team at that medal's typical regional line (problems only).
- **\*** Rated from the onsite XCPCIO board: the onsite teams' fitted abilities are held fixed and mapped onto the shipped scale; the medal line is ranked among the linked onsite teams and compared with the regionals ranked the same way. QOJ: CCPC category https://qoj.ac/category/205 (2023: https://qoj.ac/category/275).
