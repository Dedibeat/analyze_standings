ICPC East Asia — medal badges

Medal cutoffs are each contest's official medal counts from its XCPCIO board (2022 regionals: a fixed 35 gold / 70 silver / 105 bronze; later seasons ≈ gold 10%, silver 30%, bronze 60% cumulative of official teams solving at least one problem). Each problem is badged with the weakest tier whose boundary team solves it at ≥50% odds, measured on standings-fitted difficulties (hierarchical binary model with solve-time slices, Codeforces-equivalent points). Above the gold bar sit two more tiers, split at the champion cohort (the top-5 official teams): still solvable by the champions at even odds → platinum (decides ranking within gold); beyond even the champions → ★ star. Official teams are recovered from the mixed qoj standings by name-matching the XCPCIO board's official teams (star teams excluded).

Generated by arch_b.export_medal_viewer from output/medal_badges.json (analysis: arch_b.medals). Caveats:
  • Medal counts are the boards' own; only Hong Kong & Macau 2022 (no counts on its board) uses ceil rounding of the 10/30/60% cutoffs. Name matching can admit a few same-named unofficial rows (at most 4 per contest), which can shift a cutoff team by a rank or two.
  • The domjudge field is taken as the medal-eligible field — fuzzy for COVID-era online contests (Hong Kong & Macau 2022's “official” field includes non-EA online teams, which is why its gold bar is the outlier).
  • Xi'an 2025 was declared unrated; it is included with its qoj standings as-is.
  • A model-free sanity badge (weakest medal band where ≥50% of teams solved; top-5 majority splits platinum/star) agrees on ~74% of problems; disagreements are almost all one step easier, as expected when anchoring on the band majority instead of the boundary team.
  • The star bar leans on a 5-team champion cohort, so it is the noisiest of the four bars — in small fields (e.g. Hong Kong 2024) it can sit close to the gold bar and badge a several-solve problem ★.
