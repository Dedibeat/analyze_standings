## Problems

### A. Absolute Difference — field 113 (QOJ) — not attempted (no code)
- **Idea:** the expected |x−y| over unions of intervals. Non-overlapping interval pairs separate into
  closed forms, summed with prefix sums. Overlapping pairs (only O(n+m) of them) are split into an
  equal part and ≤ 3 disjoint parts. Degenerate sets (all points) use weight 1 instead of length.
  Tags: math/integration, prefix sums, careful casework. The standard 5th problem.

### B. Binary Substrings — 6; G. Meet in the Middle — 11; J. Referee Without Red — 4; K. Security at Museums — 4; M. Vulpecula — 1
- Not attempted. Correct skips (de Bruijn construction, tree-diameter segment trees, permutation
  parity, polygon visibility DP, linear-basis intersections).

### C. Clamped Sequence — field 829 — **AC 24.9' after 1 WA** (Erdene)
- **Idea:** r = l + d. The sum is piecewise linear in l with breakpoints at the a_i (and a_i − d), so
  try O(n) candidate l: O(n²). Tags: enumeration of breakpoints.
- 3136338 (21.9') WA: tried only `l = a[i]`. 3136373 (24.9') adds `r = a[i]; l = r - d;` → AC.
  A missing symmetric case; caught by thinking "an optimum lies at a breakpoint of either end".

### D. DRX vs. T1 — field 907 — **AC 5.7', first try** (Tamir). Simulation.

### E. Graph Completing — field 57 — not attempted
- Bridge-tree plus inclusion–exclusion over uncovered tree edges, tree knapsack O(n²). A 6th-problem
  candidate.

### F. Half Mixed — field 295 — **AC 215.6', first try** (idea Erdene, implementation Dedi)
- **Idea:** total subrectangles must be even. Then solve the 1×m (or n×1) version, splitting m into
  runs with Σl_i(l_i+1)/2 = m(m+1)/4, greedily taking the largest possible run, and stack it.
  Tags: constructive, greedy.
- **Ours:** 3138576 is exactly that (`construct(n)` greedy with `assert(pure * 2 == tot)`), then
  rows or columns. Clean, with asserts that guard the construction. But it was finished 120 minutes
  after the field's median.

### H. P-P-Palindrome — field 46; I. Quartz Collection — field 81 — not attempted
- I: by profit b_i − a_i, sort and assign alternately; maintain with a value segment tree under
  updates. Data structures plus a greedy game argument; the second 5th-problem option.

### L. Tavern Chess — field 552 — **AC 152.0' after 1 WA** (Erdene)
- **Idea:** brute-force recursion over all battle states (at most (7!)² states). Tags: brute force,
  probability.
- 3137798 (142.7') WA: correct recursion, but the output was `printf("%.10.lf\n", alice);`, a
  malformed format string. 3137919 (152.0') `"%.10lf"` → AC.
- **Lesson:** compile with `-Wall` (it warns on this format) and look at the sample output before
  submitting: the sample would have printed garbage.
