### C. Permutation Counting 4 — field 308 — **AC 68.5', first try** (Dedi)
- **Idea (from our code):** permanent mod 2 = determinant mod 2. Interval rows can be eliminated
  greedily: for each l take the row with the smallest r; two equal smallest r give a determinant of 0;
  the other rows become [r+1, …] and merge into l' = r+1 (small-to-large heap merge). Tags: linear
  algebra mod 2, mergeable heaps.
- **Ours:** 2800791, exactly that elimination. A strong first-try solve of an "insight" problem.

### F. Make Max — field 1564 — **AC 53.1' after 1 WA** (Tamir)
- **Idea:** each element contributes (span where it is the strict max) − 1 via monotonic stacks.
  Equal values must be cut off on one side.
- 2800660 WA: stacks with `<` on both sides double-count equal values. 2800695 adds
  `if(a[i] == a[prv[i]]) prv[i] = i - 1;` → AC. **Bug class:** ties in monotonic stacks.

### G. The Median of the Median of the Median — field 497 — **AC 174.1' after 2 failed runs** (Erdene)
- **Idea:** binary search the answer m. Map a_i to ±1 (≥ m or not); b_{l,r} ≥ m iff the prefix-sum
  difference is > 0; count the pairs where c_{l,r} ≥ m with an O(n²) sweep; compare with half of all
  pairs. Tags: binary search on the answer, median ±1 trick.
- **Attempts:**
  1. 2801138 (119.6') "AC, WA": the method is right, but the final test was
     `sum >= (n * (n + 1) / 2 + 1) / 2`.
  2. 2801280 (146.3') same verdict: **blind change**, adding only `#define int long long` (no
     overflow existed: n = 2000).
  3. 2801432 (174.1') AC: `int tot = n * (n + 1) / 2; if(sum > tot / 2)`, i.e. the off-by-one in
     "the median is the ⌈m/2⌉-th smallest" under the ≥ m transform.
- **Lesson:** median definitions (⌈m/2⌉-th) with "≥ m" counting are a classic off-by-one. Brute-force
  the triple median for n ≤ 8 and compare.

### L. Bull Farm — field 125 — **AC 240.7' after 4 WA; bug found with GPT** (idea Dedi, implementation Tamir)
- **Idea (from our code):** process buttons in order. A permutation button merges components; a
  button with exactly one repeated target adds one directed edge. Maintain reachability (DSU for
  mutual reachability, SCC/bitset closure) and answer queries offline by c. Tags: graphs,
  incremental transitive closure.
- **Attempts:** 2801643–2801842 (208–235'): fixes to counting the "exactly one collision" case
  (`xxa`, `t != 2`) and to where `G.build()` is called. 2801894 (240.7') AC adds, in the DSU union,
  the missing edges into the SCC structure (`scc.addEdge(u, v); scc.addEdge(v, u); g[u].pb(v);
  g[v].pb(u);`).
- **Interpretation:** a 6 KB custom structure built in contest; the decisive bug (merges not
  propagated to the SCC graph) was not found by the team. This is not evidence of in-contest
  ability. It does show the risk of hand-rolled incremental-graph code without a tested component.
