### C. Canvas Painting — field 356 — **AC 168.7', first try**
- **Idea (from our code):** sweep positions, keep the spells covering the current position in a
  min-heap by right end, and use the spell that ends soonest to merge position i into i+1. The answer
  is n − merges. Tags: greedy interval scheduling, heap. Jumps over gaps with `mp.lower_bound`
  (n ≤ 10^9).

### M. Teleporter — field 694 — **AC 110.9' after 2 WA**
- **Idea:** layered relaxation. For k = 1..n: q[u] = min d[v] over teleport neighbours v. Relax q
  along the tree **bottom-up, then top-down in the same array**, then d = min(d, q). O(n(n+m)).
  Tags: shortest paths on a tree plus extra edges, DP over the number of teleports.
- **Attempts:**
  1. 2862338 (97.9') WA: relaxed in two **separate** arrays, `forw` (preorder) and `bck`
     (postorder), then took `min({d[i], forw[i], bck[i]})`. A path that goes up to an ancestor and
     then down into another subtree is never composed.
  2. 2862373 (100.3') WA: skips the parent edge in each pass. Same structural bug.
  3. 2862511 (110.9') AC: one array `q`, `revord` (child → parent) then `ord` (parent → child).
- **Lesson:** a tree "distance from the nearest source" needs the up pass and the down pass
  composed. A brute force with Dijkstra on 6-node trees would have caught it.
