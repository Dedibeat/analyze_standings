## Problems

### A. Bridge — field 52 (QOJ) — not attempted
- Sqrt decomposition over queries: each block of B queries touches ≤ 2B countries; build a B×B
  permutation table per block. O(n√n). Tags: sqrt decomposition, simulation. 8th-problem level.

### B. Cells Coloring — field 102 (QOJ) — **not attempted; the 7th problem**
- **Idea:** cells not colored 0 are edges of a bipartite graph (rows × columns). With k colors this
  is possible iff the max degree ≤ k (König's edge-colouring theorem). So for each k, delete the fewest
  edges to make max degree ≤ k: a b-matching / max flow with capacity k per row and column. Increase
  k and add capacity on the residual network. Answer: min over k of c·k + d·(empty − flow). Tags:
  bipartite edge coloring, max flow.
- **Why missed (likely):** it needs (1) the König edge-colouring fact and (2) an incremental max-flow
  template. Neither appears anywhere in our code this season. **Question:** was B read? Does the
  team have a tested Dinic template?

### E. Find Maximum — field 325 (QOJ) — **AC 123.8' after 2 WA**
- **Idea:** f(x) = (sum of base-3 digits) + (number of base-3 digits). Maximise over [l, r] with a
  digit DP (or a greedy on the base-3 prefix). Tags: digit DP, math.
- **Attempts:**
  1. 3072945 (12.1') WA, **assumption**: computes only f(r) (`int cur = r; while(cur > 0) …`).
     The maximum over the range is not at r in general: for [5, 9], f(8) = 6 > f(9) = 4. All ten
     samples happen to have the maximum at r, so they could not catch it.
  2. 3073659 (112.2') WA: a recursive `comp(l, r)` on base-3 structure with a boundary error
     (`comp((l + 1) / 3, r / 3) + 3`, and no `l == r` base case).
  3. 3073765 (123.8') AC: fixes the boundaries (`comp(l / 3, r / 3)`, `if(l == r) return f(l)`) and
     adds `test()` against a brute force.
- **Lesson:** the first WA came 12 minutes in on an unproven "max is at r" assumption, a 5-line
  brute force away from being refuted. The tester that found the final bug was written only after
  the second WA.

### G. Perfect Word — field 466 — **AC 48.2', first try**
- **Idea:** t is perfect iff both of its length-(|t|−1) prefix and suffix are perfect. Process the

### L. Tree — field 266 (QOJ) — **AC 91.4', first try**
- **Idea:** cover by chains and antichains. Every antichain peels the current leaves, so the answer
  is min_j (cnt_j + j − 1) over leaf-peeling layers. Ours (3073541) peels leaves with a set and takes
  `min(ans, cur + leaf.size())`: the editorial's formula.
