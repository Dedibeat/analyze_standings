## Problems

### A. Recall — field 1019 — **AC 240' after 4 wrong tries**: Dedi's 4 WAs, then Erdene took over, AC first try
- **Idea:** each push copy must survive until its last successful query (need_i). Scan left to right
  and pop the stack top whenever its need has passed. Greedy plus an exchange argument; O(n) with
  hashing. Tags: greedy, stack, constructive.
- No code, so Dedi's 4 wrong tries cannot be checked. His own account: **a wrong idea, or an
  implementation too complicated to get right** (user, 2026-11-05). A constructive greedy with an
  exchange-argument proof is the problem type where unproven variants get submitted (cf. 2524 C,
  1053 A).
- **The handover is the lesson:** a teammate's fresh solution went in first try after four failed
  runs. The rule "2 WAs → hand it over" (summary §6.3) would have triggered it two runs, and
  40 penalty minutes, earlier.

### B. Mod — 0; E. LCM Permutation — 0; I. A Lamp of Moon — 1; K. Wolf Game — 15

### C. Permutation Inversions — field 1587 — **AC 74', first try** (Dedi)
- Build edges q1→q2→…; Kahn's topological sort with a min-heap of positions gives the
  inversion-minimal permutation (special to interval constraints). Tags: graphs, topological sort.

### D. Sequence — field 704 — **AC 180' after 1 wrong try** (Tamir)
- Keep counts (a, b) of zeros and ones normalised a ≤ b. When a = b both choices give p = a (×2);
  when a < b the choice is forced. The answer is 2^k or 0. Tags: counting, greedy simulation.

### G. Toll Gates on a Tight Schedule — 135; H. The First Problem — 57; J. String — 120

### L. Longest Common Prefix — field 1039 — **AC 258', first try** (Dedi)
- **Idea:** insert strings into a trie. When a depth-h node's count first reaches cnt (> g[h]),
  set f[cnt] = h. Keep Σ f[j] ⊕ j incrementally. O(total length). Tags: trie, strings,
  monotone structure.
- A trie problem with a short intended solution, solved late.
