**How each round was read:**
- **The field.** The full official standings of the round.
- **The same-set tier.** Official contestants who solved exactly our problems; our place among
  them shows speed.
- **The next tier.** Contestants who solved our problems plus exactly one more.
  - Their median minute on each of our problems shows where we were slow.
  - The extra problem most of them solved is **the field's next problem for us**: the problem that
    would have moved us up.
- **The editorial** of that next problem was read to name its type. The editorials come from
  `../codeforces_integration`; 13 of the 99 were missing.
- **Virtual entries** are placed in the official standings by recomputing our score.
  - The recomputation is exact on all 13 ICPC-rules rated entries and within 27 points on CF-rules
    ones.
  - It also showed that Codeforces does not count test-1 failures (see `cf.md`).

Problem ids are `<contest><letter>`; ratings in parentheses.
