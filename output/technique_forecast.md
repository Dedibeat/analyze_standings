# Techniques in ICPC Asia East regionals: 2022-2025 and a 2026 forecast

Generated by `scripts/technique_forecast.py`. Data: 27 ICPC EC regionals (2022-2025, 346 problems) and 8 CCPC contests (2024-2025 sites and finals). Named techniques: Gemini extraction (`data/techniques_atlas.json`, 128-id taxonomy; primary or secondary tag). General tags: DeepSeek `primary_tags` (ICPC only; the CCPC problems have none). Difficulty: calibrated CF-scale `difficulty_cf`; *position* = rank by difficulty inside the contest (1 = easiest).

**Key numbers**

- A regional has 12.8 problems; 7.2 (56%) use no named technique. Only 10% of positions 1-3 need one.
- ICPC regionals 2025: log loss 0.2169 vs uniform 0.2368 (gain 0.0199, 90% CI 0.0095 to 0.0305); the top-20 list covers 39% of named problems (random list 18%).
- CCPC 2024-2025: log loss 0.2356 vs uniform 0.2574 (gain 0.0218, 90% CI 0.0184 to 0.0250); the top-20 list covers 42% of named problems (random list 18%).
- No trend survives Holm (smallest Holm p 1.000), nor any ICPC-vs-CCPC difference (0.524). Site-specific forecasts beat the pooled one in 1 of 8 (season, beta) cases.
- Derived categories catch 15% of DeepSeek's dp problems and 16% of its math problems, so general dp / greedy / math are not compared with CCPC.
- 2026, most likely per regional: dsu 25%, fenwick 25%, fft 23%, convex-hull 21%, rolling-hash 21%.

## 2. Held-out tests

Model: recency-weighted presence rate per technique, shrunk toward its category's mean rate. Tuned on 2022-2023 -> 2024: alpha = 16, decay = 1.0 (log loss 0.2474; over decay 0.25-1.0 at this alpha it spans 0.2474-0.2512, so recency hardly matters). Then fitted on the 2022-2024 regionals and scored on every (contest, technique) cell of both test sets. Uniform = one rate for every technique; category-only = each technique gets its category's mean rate.

| test | contests | log loss model | category-only | uniform | gain vs uniform (90% CI) | Brier model / uniform | named problems whose techniques were unseen in training |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| ICPC regionals 2025 | 7 | 0.2169 | 0.2349 | 0.2368 | 0.0199 (0.0095 to 0.0305) | 0.0564 / 0.0596 | 18% of 38 |
| CCPC 2024-2025 | 8 | 0.2356 | 0.2544 | 0.2574 | 0.0218 (0.0184 to 0.0250) | 0.0626 / 0.0663 | 16% of 50 |

Coverage: share of test problems with a named primary technique that is in the model's top-N list (a random top-N list in brackets).

| test | top 10 | top 20 | top 30 |
|---|---|---|---|
| ICPC regionals 2025 | 26% (9%) | 39% (18%) | 53% (27%) |
| CCPC 2024-2025 | 24% (9%) | 42% (18%) | 54% (26%) |

Calibration (forecast bin: cells, mean forecast vs observed rate):

- ICPC regionals 2025: < 0.05: 399 cells, 0.029 vs 0.028; < 0.10: 315 cells, 0.060 vs 0.054; < 0.20: 161 cells, 0.137 vs 0.155; < 1.01: 21 cells, 0.230 vs 0.190
- CCPC 2024-2025: < 0.05: 456 cells, 0.029 vs 0.026; < 0.10: 360 cells, 0.060 vs 0.069; < 0.20: 184 cells, 0.137 vs 0.158; < 1.01: 24 cells, 0.230 vs 0.292

Adding CCPC 2024 to the 2022-2024 training set changes the ICPC 2025 log loss 0.2169 -> 0.2166.

Site effect: log loss on test regionals whose site hosted before, pooled forecast vs one shrunk toward the site's own past regionals (beta = weight of the pooled forecast):

- 2024 (Hangzhou, Hong Kong, Nanjing, Shenyang): pooled 0.2407; beta=2 0.2761, beta=4 0.2586, beta=8 0.2490, beta=16 0.2444
